Terms

Dapperowl CIC connects experts with organisations ("firms"). Firms donate to the expert's chosen charity. Dapperowl never receives, holds or passes on that money.

  1. Experts must be 18 or over, and their profile details must be true.
  2. Firms must use a verified work email and website.
  3. Donations are paid by the firm directly to the charity, within 48 hours of the expert pressing Send.
  4. Experts give personal views, not professional advice.
  5. A firm that does not pay, without flagging a problem, may be removed from the site.
  6. We may remove any profile or account that breaks these terms or the code of conduct.

Privacy

We keep only what we need to run the service: profile details, account emails, requests and donation receipts.

  • Expert email and phone are never shown publicly; they're shared with a firm only after the expert accepts its request.
  • A donation total is visible only to the expert who earned it.
  • Firm ratings are private and seen only by Dapperowl.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@dapperowl.co.uk.

Code of conduct

Experts tick these before every engagement

  1. I won't share inside information: anything confidential and not public that could affect a company's value or share price.
  2. I won't share confidential information belonging to a current or former employer or client, or anything I've promised to keep secret.
  3. I don't currently work for, advise or sit on the board of the organisation this engagement is about, or I've told the firm that I do.
  4. Taking part doesn't break any agreement I'm bound by, and my employer has agreed where that's needed.
  5. If I work in the public sector, I'm allowed to take part.
  6. I can decline any question, and I'll stop the conversation if it moves into these areas.

Firms agree to these with every request

  1. We won't ask for inside information, or for anything the expert isn't free to share.
  2. We'll respect the expert's right to decline a question or end the conversation.
  3. Within 48 hours of the expert pressing Send, we'll donate the agreed amount directly to the charity or flag a problem.
  4. We'll use the expert's contact details only for this engagement.
  5. We won't record the conversation unless the expert agrees.
  6. The expert is giving a personal view, not professional advice. Our decisions are our own responsibility.
